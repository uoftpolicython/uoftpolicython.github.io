Policython

Policython is a multi-day conference that challenges undergraduate and graduate students from universities across Canada to propose policy recommendations on pressing societal issues. Participants dig into real policy problems, develop evidence-based recommendations, and present them to a panel of judges.

About Policython

Students deserve a seat at the table.

Our aim

The University of Toronto Policython pursues one vision: to equip youth with the skills and experiences needed to excel in policymaking, governance, and advocacy.

The conference combines the educational depth of Model UN with the fast pace of a hackathon, so students practise real policy work: research, analysis, and persuasion.

Although Policython is UTP’s headline event, we offer various programming and workshops focused on policymaking, political engagement, and networking. UTP’s events include Fireside Chats with industry professionals, policy writing workshops, and social events. Follow us on our Instagram to stay updated on upcoming events.

About Veritas

Veritas is the University of Toronto Policython’s official journal. Rooted in the pursuit of truth, Veritas reflects our commitment to a rigorous and analytical exploration of public policy and its multifaceted impacts on our world. Through Veritas, we seek to uphold UTP’s legacy of fostering excellence in research and advancing policy literacy within the undergraduate academic community.
